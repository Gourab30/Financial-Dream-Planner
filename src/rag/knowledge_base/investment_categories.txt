Broad Investment Category Guide (Educational only - no individual stocks,
no guaranteed returns, not a recommendation to buy any specific product)

Short-term category (goal is 3 years away or less):
This project recommends a capital-preservation oriented category for
short-term goals, such as debt-oriented mutual funds, fixed deposits or
liquid funds. These typically prioritise protecting the invested amount
over maximising growth, because there is little time to recover from a
market downturn before the goal is due.

Medium-term category (goal is 4 to 7 years away):
This project recommends a diversified balanced category for medium-term
goals, such as hybrid mutual funds that mix equity and debt. This aims to
balance growth potential with reduced volatility compared to a pure
equity portfolio.

Long-term category (goal is more than 7 years away):
This project recommends a diversified long-term growth oriented category
for long-term goals, such as diversified equity mutual funds. Longer time
horizons generally allow more time to ride out short-term market
volatility in pursuit of higher long-term growth.

Important limits of this project:
- The system never recommends an individual stock, fund house or ticker.
- The system never guarantees a rate of return; expected_investment_return
  is a user-adjustable assumption, not a promise.
- These categories are broad educational buckets only, intended to teach
  the concept of matching investment risk to time horizon.
