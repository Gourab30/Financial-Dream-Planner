City-Wise Cost Differences (Educational only)

Why goal costs differ by city: The base cost of a Marriage, New Car or
New Home goal varies by city mainly because of differences in local
income levels, real-estate prices, and the general cost of goods and
services. Metro cities such as Mumbai, Delhi and Bangalore tend to have
higher goal costs than other cities in the dataset, largely driven by
higher real-estate and venue costs.

How a city's base cost is calculated: The underlying dataset records
goal costs separately for several area types within each city (for
example Central, North, South and Premium localities). Since a user only
supplies a city name and not a specific area, this project averages the
cost across all recorded area types for that city to produce one
representative base cost per goal.

Supported cities list: Ahmedabad, Bangalore, Chennai, Delhi, Hyderabad,
Jaipur, Kolkata, Lucknow, Mumbai, Pune.

Metro vs non-metro planning: Because salaries are also predicted using
city as an input, a higher-cost metro city does not automatically mean a
goal becomes harder to reach - the salary prediction for that same city
is also usually higher, and the actual feasibility depends on the ratio
between the required SIP and the predicted available capacity, not the
raw cost alone.
